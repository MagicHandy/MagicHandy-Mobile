# MagicHandy Mobile v0.1.6

## Changelog

- First public release of MagicHandy Mobile for adults 18 and older. On Android 12 or later, download and open `MagicHandy-0.1.6.apk` under **Assets**. The matching checksum is in `SHA256SUMS`.
- On first launch, try Stop with nothing connected. During a session, use Stop from the app, notification or Quick Settings tile. The receipt shows which devices confirmed.
- Chat with a built-in or custom companion using ChatGPT, OpenRouter or a supported server on your local network. Choose conversation only, use manual controls, or let Autopilot change the pace within your limits.
- Connect The Handy over Bluetooth, Handy Cloud or Intiface Central. Other supported devices include Intiface strokers and vibrators, DG-LAB Coyote 3.0, Autoblow Ultra and VacuGlide.
- VacuGlide can keep running if the connection drops. Switch it off at the device. Intiface answers Stop on behalf of its devices, so check the device when the receipt shows an unconfirmed stop.
- The owner tested MagicHandy Test 0.1.6 with ChatGPT on a physical Pixel 10 Pro XL and accepted its performance for this release.
- If you used MagicHandy Test, the public app installs alongside it. Open **MagicHandy**, sign in again and add your devices. Each app keeps its own chats and settings.
