# MagicHandy Mobile v0.1.8

## Changelog

- **Talk instead of typing.** With nothing typed, tap the microphone beside the message field. The first time, **Download and start** fetches a 671 MB speech model; Wi-Fi is best. Each thing you say is sent when you pause, as if you'd typed it, and listening carries on with the screen off. Tap **End** when you're done.
- Your voice stays on the phone. It's turned into text here, by Parakeet v3, and never saved or sent. Your chat model gets only the text.
- **Say "Stop" to stop every device.** A spoken Stop goes straight to Stop, before chat sees the words. It works in English, Spanish and Portuguese. The Stop button is still the fastest way.
- **Any Stop ends listening**, and nothing you said before it is sent. Listening also ends after 15 minutes without speech while nothing is playing.
- Phones with 12 GB of memory or more can choose **Full precision** (2.6 GB) instead, under **Choose another model** or later in **App settings › Voice**. It's slower and uses about 3.6 GB of memory while you talk.
- **App settings › Voice** shows the speech model, switches it, removes it from the phone and links to microphone access.
- Voice needs a phone with at least 6 GB of memory. It isn't offered while the app is in Japanese or Chinese, where spoken Stop doesn't work yet.
- Android asks for the microphone the first time. While the app listens, its notification says so and offers **End**, if notifications are allowed.
- Voice input was checked on the Pixel 10 emulator with simulated devices. It hasn't been tested on a phone in this build; the voice test build on a Pixel 10 Pro XL settled how it listens.
- The APK grows from 5 MB to about 16 MB, since it now carries the speech engine for 64-bit ARM phones.
- Install `MagicHandy-0.1.8.apk` over 0.1.7 to keep your chats, settings and devices. The matching checksum is in `SHA256SUMS`.
