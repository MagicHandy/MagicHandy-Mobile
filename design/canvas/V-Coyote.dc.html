Coyote 3.0

Before you connect
1Do not use it with a pacemaker or other implant, a heart condition, epilepsy, or during pregnancy.
2Place electrodes below the waist only. Never across the chest or near the heart.
3Start at zero and raise strength slowly. MagicHandy never raises strength on its own.
4Stop sets both channels to zero and ends the pulses.

Turn the Coyote on and close its own app first. You'll pick it from a list of nearby devices; it never connects on its own.
