Design directions for chat, Autopilot and devices on Android. Sketches, not specs.

Stop never movesSame spot on every screen, in thumb reach, at least 56 dp. Red only for Stop. Also on the notification, lock screen and Quick Settings.
Thumb zone firstSession controls live in the lower half. Anything changed once a session or less sits in one compact settings menu.
Glance, then actOne-line Now playing. Status is a dot plus a word. Commanded values say they are commanded.
Dark by defaultBuilt for dim rooms and OLED screens. No glow, no flashing, reduced motion respected.
Honest under failureUnconfirmed Stop, a lost link or held motion is said plainly, with one next step.
Survives AndroidA foreground service keeps runs alive; if Android closes the app anyway, the next launch says what happened.
