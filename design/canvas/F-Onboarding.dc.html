Before you start

Adults only

MagicHandy controls intimate devices. Use it responsibly and at your own risk.

Stop never moves

The red bar at the bottom stops every device, on every screen. It is also on the notification, the lock screen and in Quick Settings.

Try it now

Press Stop below. Nothing is connected yet, so it answers "No connected device to stop." It is the same button in the same place every time.

MagicHandy asks for Bluetooth, notification and battery access when a feature first needs it, and says why.
